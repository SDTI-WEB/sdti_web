Disclosure of Hazardous Waste Information

  • Company Name： Super Dragon Environmental Protection Technology (Suzhou) Limited Company
  • Address： No. 37, Dongfu Road, Suzhou Industrial Park, Jiangsu Province
  • Legal Representative and Contact Number： Yao-Hsun Wu Tel: +86-512-65933338
  • Fang Chao Yang　Tel: +86-151-5018-9392
  • Hazardous Waste Management License No.： JSSZ050000D059-2
  • Validity Period of License：From October 8, 2022 to October 7, 2023
  • Approved Categories and Handling Capacity of Hazardous Waste：
    Disposal and utilization of HW49 (Other Wastes) — limited to 900-045-49 waste printed circuit boards without electronic components, with an annual capacity of 4,000 tons. Treatment and recovery of HW16 (Photographic Material Waste – Used Fixing Solution) and precious-metal-containing waste liquids, including HW17 (Surface Treatment Waste Liquids), HW33 (Inorganic Cyanide Waste Liquids), HW34 (Waste Acids), and HW35 (Alkaline Waste Liquids) — with a combined annual capacity of 500 tons. Reception of high-concentration palladium waste liquids is strictly prohibited.
  • Secondary Hazardous Waste Categories and Quantities： Organic Resin Powder (HW13): 0.5 tons Sludge (HW22): 10 tons
  • Environmental Pollution Prevention Measures ：
    1.Independent storage facilities are provided.
    2.Professional and clear labeling is implemented.
    3.Preventive measures against rain, seepage, and fire are in place.
    4.The storage area is strictly supervised and monitored 24 hours a day to prevent leaks and environmental contamination.
