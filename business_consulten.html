Environmental Consulting Services

SDTI fully recognizes that in promoting environmental protection, safety, and occupational health, it must maintain 100% compliance with all relevant laws and regulations.
Our Environmental Protection, Safety, and Health Policy is founded on the goal of achieving environmental sustainability, circular economy, and zero-accident safety performance, positioning SDTI as both an environmental pioneer and a benchmark enterprise for workplace safety and hygiene.
To achieve these objectives, SDTI implements a strategic approach based on the principles of legal compliance, pollution prevention, continuous improvement, strengthening resource circularity, managing EHS (Environment, Health, and Safety) risks, and fostering an EHS-driven corporate culture to realize the vision of “EHS for Everyone.”
All SDTI plants have obtained certification for the Environmental Management System (ISO 14001:2004) and the Occupational Health and Safety Management System (OHSAS 18001:2007).
Upholding a spirit of continuous improvement, SDTI actively enhances its performance in environmental protection, pollution prevention, waste reduction, and occupational safety and hygiene management. Through these ongoing efforts, we aim to minimize overall EHS risks, achieve zero accidents, and set an example as an industry leader and a model of social responsibility.
