Precious Metal Recovery and Processin

Major Industries for Precious Metal Recycling

IC semiconductor industry & packaging and testing industry

Scrap ICs, scrap chips, mixed hardware, copper-plated silver, nickel-plated silver, copper scraps, nickel scraps, waste silver liquid, waste tin slag, waste silver slag, waste plastics, resins, etc.

Computer Peripheral Manufacturing Industry

Waste computers, monitors (CRTs), printers, PCBs, degaussing yokes, wires, cables, and dismantled components

PC Board Manufacturing and Processing Industry

Scrap boards, waste PCBA, gold-plated boards, tin edge trims, copper edge trims, copper substrates, aluminum sheets, and copper foils.

Precision Electronics, Optoelectronics, Telecommunications, and Related Industries

Waste computers, monitors (CRTs), printers, PCBs, degaussing yokes, wires, cables, and dismantled components
